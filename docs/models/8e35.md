**Flashless >115 W GPU TGP investigation — 25 November 2026 (driver `621.14`, VBIOS `98.06.2a.40.da`, Secure Boot ON, Windows Defender 100% untouched).** Read-only disassembly of `nvlddmkm.sys` (`621.14`) confirmed that stock Monolithic RM loads persistent `HKLM\SYSTEM\CurrentControlSet\Control\Class\{4d36e968-e325-11ce-bfc1-08002be10318}\0000\vbios` (`REG_BINARY`) on GPU restart without requiring `EnableGpuFirmware`, Test-Signing, or any unsigned driver, and caches the untouched `465,664`-byte stock ROM (`SHA-256 C7082786D9E4A063EC249BEA8FE8ECAF69054C0EF2CFEB0B46F3C393F44E11CB`) in volatile `0000\Session\vbios`. Coherently shifting `Policy[2]` (`0x57B1B`, `PWR_POLICY_TOTAL_GPU`) at `0x57B2C` (`limitRated`), `0x57B30` (`limitMax`), `0x57B48` (`inflection[0]`), and `0x57B64` (`tgpRated`), restarting the dGPU (`pnputil /restart-device`), and re-sending HP WMI Unleashed (`0x1A/0x22/0x29`, required because GPU restart resets `nvlddmkm`'s cTGP state to `limitRated - 15 W`) unlocked both **125 W** (+10 W: `90 / 125 W`, `119.12 W` steady / `131.16 W` peak) and **135 W** (+20 W: `100 / 135 W`, `124.52 W` steady avg across 23 samples vs `114.11 W` stock baseline on the identical 4K Aniso+MRT load, with bursts to **`140.30–142.04 W`**, `71.7 °C` max, throttle shifted from `SW Power Cap 0x04` to `Vrel 0x400`, `PnP = OK`, `0` Defender alerts). Full rollback after both trials (deleting `0000\vbios`, restarting the dGPU, re-sending HP Unleashed) restored exact stock `80 W / 115 W`. Detailed offsets and caveats are recorded in [NVPWRCONTROL_TGP_FORK_PLAN.md](../plans/NVPWRCONTROL_TGP_FORK_PLAN.md).

In Balanced, PPAB (Dynamic Boost) lowers the enforced limit from the 80 W default to 65 W; cTGP and TPP do not change it. Performance and Unleashed were measured only with cTGP and PPAB on.

**NVIDIA platform controller (NPCF, `NVDA0820`), read-only ACPI analysis, 30 October 2026.** The device is in SSDT `HPQOEM 8E35` (registry `SSD4`, 14 472 bytes); its `_DSM` sub-function 2 returns the Dynamic Boost parameters in 1/8 W units. The values depend on the low nibble of EC register `R950` (EC-maintained, not read here; consistent with the low nibble of the mode byte the BIOS stores in EC `HPCM` via `0x1A`):

| `R950 & 0x0F` | cTGP offset (`TGPA`, only when WMI cTGP = 1) | TPP (`TPPA`) | Dynamic Boost range |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 or 4 | +20 W (`ACBT` 0xA0) | 45 W (`ATPP` 0x168), replaced by the `0x29` TPP (`DATP`) when set | +15 / −15 W (`AMAT` 0x78, `AMIT` 0xFF88) |
| any other | 0 | 30 W (`ATPP` 0xF0), `0x29` TPP ignored | +15 / −15 W |

`0x22` PPAB = 0 sets `DBAC` = 1 (Dynamic Boost disabled on AC); EC queries `_Q79`/`_Q7A` set `DBDC` for DC; every change notifies NPCF. Battery values (`TGPD`, `TPPD`) are 0. With the 80 W driver default this matches the measurement: Balanced (nibble 0) enforces 80 − 15 = 65 W with Dynamic Boost and 80 W without, and ignores cTGP and TPP; Performance 0x31 with cTGP on and 45 W TPP enforced 80 + 20 − 15 = 85 W; Unleashed 0x04 with 72 W TPP moved within 85…115 W (driver maximum 115 W). On this table, Performance (and the Hub's L5 0x11 / L6 0x21) uses the same envelope as Unleashed; only TPP, PL1 and the fan behaviour differ. This is an inference from the table; Performance with a raised TPP was not measured. Consequence (user decision): a preset with Dynamic Boost on applies Unleashed before its own values, off applies Balanced ([custom presets](../CUSTOM_PRESETS.md)).

## CPU and Windows evidence

25 November crash follow-up: the installed driver's GPU-scope readback showed an already loaded 135 W maximum and an unavailable configured power request. The manual path uses the existing qualified-device writer and an isolated post-restart readback. Preflight on this laptop found the owned source ready for 125 W and confirmed the existing 135 W without scheduling a restart. The app-reader regression, checks and verification scope are recorded in [Validation](../VALIDATION.md#gpu-power-apply-incident--25-november-2026).

The fixed verified PawnIO/AMDFamily17 reader measured package-energy mean watts, labeled Tctl and all 32 logical-CPU effective clocks. Windows MHz remains separate. [CPU sensors](../CPU_SENSORS.md) holds exact driver/module identities.

Independent limits use fixed RSMU messages and PM-table readback: PPT 0x56/cell 2, Slow 0xCB/cell 4, TDC 0x57/cell 8, EDC 0x58/cell 62, Tctl 0x59/cell 10. Small decreases, CO around −20 and PBO 1× had full restore. **0x540108 was an earlier transcription error**; independent STAPM was removed after coupled HP behavior failed its contract.

**Fast limit (PPT) in Unleashed — 20 November 2026, 8E35 / F.13 only.** `preset-hold.log` from 12 to 20 November holds about 2,200 PPT rewrites; cell 2 read back 80 W after every 75 W or 100 W write, typically within 1–5 s, while Slow (0x29 byte 0) and Tctl stayed put. HPOmenCap and HPSysInfoCap were stopped/disabled and the HP `HpReadHWData` driver was absent, so the OMEN Hub (1101.2611.2.0, decompiled read-only) had no write route; its AMD code never writes a fast limit (`ProcessPL4`/`HandlingPL4` return on non-Intel). The F.13 DSDT `GM29` handler maps 0x29 byte 0 → EC OSPT (slow) and byte 1 → OSPL; no ACPI path writes the OS fast field (OFPT) or ALIB's fast-PPT parameter. Conclusion: the firmware (EC/SMI) keeps the fast limit in Unleashed; the trigger (periodic EC logic, NVIDIA Dynamic Boost via `nvpcf` or SMI side effects) is not identified. UXTU/RyzenAdj use the same RSMU 0x56 or MP1 0x3E messages and offer no proven alternative. Since beta.8 a preset in Unleashed skips PPT without writing and names it; no PPT hold loop runs.

**Fast & Slow PPT unlock with Dynamic Boost (`nvpcf.sys` Sub-function #9 bypass) — 25 November 2026, 8E35 / F.13.** Live isolation probes (Probes #1–#3) plus read-only disassembly of `C:\Windows\System32\drivers\nvpcf.sys` (`32.0.16.2067`, SHA-256 `e443017ca11074a285b067a4fd2afbab16c44c093a8c4d26e415066e6c52989f`) and ACPI `SSD4` (`NVDA0820._DSM`) identified the exact writer: in Unleashed (`0x04`) and Performance (`0x31`) with `PPAB = 1`, `nvpcf.sys` invokes `_DSM(rev=0x200, subfunc=9)` with `CPTD = 80000 mW`, writing `EC0.NSPT = 80` and `EC0.NFPT = 80` (confirming why `PPAB = 0` in Unleashed kept Fast = 95 W locked with zero resets). In `nvpcf.sys`, `HKLM\SYSTEM\CurrentControlSet\Services\nvpcf` supports `REG_DWORD` overrides `pcfKeyConfigVersion = 0x200` and `pcfKeySupportedFuncs`. Setting `pcfKeySupportedFuncs = 0x0507` (clearing only bit 9 of ACPI's `0x0707`) skips registering the Sub-function #9 CPU-TDP overwriter while preserving Sub-functions #1, #2 (Dynamic Boost cTGP +20 W, PPAB ±15 W, TPP), #8 and #10; restarting `ACPI\NVDA0820\NPCF` via native `cfgmgr32.dll` (`CM_Disable_DevNode` / `CM_Enable_DevNode`, `CR_SUCCESS`, `problem = 0`) applies or reverts the change immediately without reboot. Measured results in Unleashed (`0x04`, `cTGP = 1`, `PPAB = 1`): `Fast = 40/70/95/100/120 W` (up to `90.0 W` measured package power) and `Slow = 30/50/55/65/80 W` (actual `30.0 W` sustained cap verified at `Slow = 30 W`; SMU caps `SetSlowLimit(90000)` at `80.0 W`) stayed 100% locked across idle, D3D11 GPU load, CPU burn, and combined load while `GM2A` `ATPP` followed `0x29` TPP (`72 W` / `55 W`), GPU enforced limit remained `115.0 W` and shifted dynamically (`115.0 → 113.3 → 109.9 W` at TPP 72 W, `102.1 → 93.1 W` at TPP 55 W). Balanced (`0x30`, `ATPP = 30`, GPU `80 W`), Quiet (`0x10`), and full rollback (deleting both registry values and restarting `ACPI\NVDA0820\NPCF`) were verified on the same session. Omen Hub++ activates this bypass automatically when applying a Dynamic Boost preset with Fast PPT or Slow PPT outside `45..=80 W`, persists the original registry state in `power-switch.json`, and restores stock `nvpcf` on Restore or stock Dashboard mode selection (and via `nvpcf-ppt-restore`).

The code permits a wider CPU range than these probes measured. Raw fuse values do not establish that whole range safe. HPOmenCap influences profile persistence: short isolation retained 70/75 W for about 12 seconds, while earlier service-present trials reverted. Full native durable ownership remains open. [System controls](../SYSTEM_CONTROLS.md), [HP control](../HP_CONTROL.md).

Windows AC/DC processor policies, target-plan originals, configured/effective overlay distinction and supported refresh were probed with restore. CCD enforcement was tested on an owned child with masks 0xFFFF / 0xFFFF0000 / original 0xFFFFFFFF, not arbitrary user programs.

## HP modes, cooling and keyboard

Factory-style Quiet/L0, Balanced, Performance and Unleashed have fixed writes and partial readback evidence. A thermal mode GET/full original is missing. Factory shortcut fallback to Balanced is not original restore. [Mode observations](8e35-hub-modes.md).

Max-Fan full flags and persisted restore were measured. The separate own helper had a brief conservative curve/Max probe reaching 4800/4800 firmware RPM. A watchdog fallback near two minutes and a thermally aborted FAMC=1 trial did not qualify direct BIOS-Auto or independent long-term cooling. [Fan control](../FAN_CONTROL.md), [handover](../FAN_HANDOVER.md).

On 17 November, software-only 0.10.0-beta.7 work added named shared fan snapshots,
manual profile links and exact preference request/recovery journals around the
existing helper. It introduces no opcode or new helper image. Isolated tests cover
interrupted file pairs, stale revisions, direct-checkpoint identity and previous
owner recovery. No live curve request, exact digest ACK or new hardware/lifecycle
qualification follows. Linked fan snapshots cannot gain sign-in authorization.

Four-zone RGB changes preserve the rest of the full 128-byte state. **0xE4 = static On**, **0x64 = Off**. Separate-process restore and actual UI readback passed; the user visibly confirmed On/Off/On on 15 October. The 16 November UI corrects zone order from the manufacturer UI assembly (0 right, 1 middle, 2 left, 3 WASD) and scales color-value brightness through RGB bytes only. Pure mapping/opaque-state tests and isolated UI interactions add software evidence, not a new device trial. General firmware brightness, animation, per-key support, exact visual zone boundaries and independent hotkey/power lifetime remain unqualified. [Lighting protocol](../KEYBOARD_LIGHTING.md).

## Startup and completion boundary

On 5 November 2026 a short GET-only diagnostic with the hash-pinned beta.2 reader confirmed PM table `0x540208`: cell 2 = 80 W PPT, cell 4 = 65 W Slow, cell 10 = 85 °C Tctl limit in all three samples. Package power was 66.1–66.6 W and Tctl 77.25–82.5 °C. Effective-clock acquisition timed out; no actual boost-clock value was established. HPOmenCap was already stopped/disabled and was not changed. This adds a current readback observation, not a new write/lifecycle qualification. [Detailed boundary](../history/RESPONSIVENESS_2026-11-05.md).

The current capability policy allows sign-in for supported lighting/Max-Fan, but does not certify their real lifecycle. New PPT/Slow sign-in needs the OEM service absent until takeover is qualified. MUX boot-preference SET/GET is not physical routing evidence.

Actual reboot, sleep/resume, AC/DC, durable HP-free operation, safe BIOS return, full allowed tuning range/load stability and a total current-helper 50-MB tray acceptance remain pending. Historical runs identify their own builds and originals; they must not be promoted to a blanket current-device safety guarantee.
