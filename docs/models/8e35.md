## CPU and Windows evidence

The fixed verified PawnIO/AMDFamily17 reader measured package-energy mean watts, labeled Tctl and all 32 logical-CPU effective clocks. Windows MHz remains separate. [CPU sensors](../CPU_SENSORS.md) holds exact driver/module identities.

Independent limits use fixed RSMU messages and PM-table readback: PPT 0x56/cell 2, Slow 0xCB/cell 4, TDC 0x57/cell 8, EDC 0x58/cell 62, Tctl 0x59/cell 10. Small decreases, CO around −20 and PBO 1× had full restore. **0x540108 was an earlier transcription error**; independent STAPM was removed after coupled HP behavior failed its contract.

The code permits a wider CPU range than these probes measured. Raw fuse values do not establish that whole range safe. HPOmenCap influences profile persistence: short isolation retained 70/75 W for about 12 seconds, while earlier service-present trials reverted. Full native durable ownership remains open. [System controls](../SYSTEM_CONTROLS.md), [HP control](../HP_CONTROL.md).

Windows AC/DC processor policies, target-plan originals, configured/effective overlay distinction and supported refresh were probed with restore. CCD enforcement was tested on an owned child with masks 0xFFFF / 0xFFFF0000 / original 0xFFFFFFFF, not arbitrary user programs.

## HP modes, cooling and keyboard

Factory-style Quiet/L0, Balanced, Performance and Unleashed have fixed writes and partial readback evidence. A thermal mode GET/full original is missing. Factory shortcut fallback to Balanced is not original restore. [Mode observations](8e35-hub-modes.md).

Max-Fan full flags and persisted restore were measured. The separate own helper had a brief conservative curve/Max probe reaching 4800/4800 firmware RPM. A watchdog fallback near two minutes and a thermally aborted FAMC=1 trial did not qualify direct BIOS-Auto or independent long-term cooling. [Fan control](../FAN_CONTROL.md), [handover](../FAN_HANDOVER.md).

Four-zone RGB changes preserve the rest of the full 128-byte state. **0xE4 = static On**, **0x64 = Off**. Separate-process restore and actual UI readback passed; the user visibly confirmed On/Off/On on 15 October. General brightness, animation, per-key support and independent hotkey/power lifetime remain unqualified. [Lighting protocol](../KEYBOARD_LIGHTING.md).

## Startup and completion boundary

The current capability policy allows sign-in for supported lighting/Max-Fan, but does not certify their real lifecycle. New PPT/Slow sign-in needs the OEM service absent until takeover is qualified. MUX boot-preference SET/GET is not physical routing evidence.

Actual reboot, sleep/resume, AC/DC, durable HP-free operation, safe BIOS return, full allowed tuning range/load stability and a total current-helper 50-MB tray acceptance remain pending. Historical runs identify their own builds and originals; they must not be promoted to a blanket current-device safety guarantee.
